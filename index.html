About the author
Sanae Mahmoud

A place for everything unsaid.

I've always found something meaningful in the things we struggle to explain.

The feelings we keep to ourselves, the memories that revisit us without warning, the people who leave impressions long after they've left our lives, and the quiet ways in which we change without even noticing.

I created A Place to Land as a space to explore these experiences. A space for writing about grief, love, identity, belonging, memory, and the many contradictions that make us human.

I don't believe every experience needs an explanation, nor do I believe every difficult feeling needs to be turned into a lesson. Sometimes, things simply happen. Sometimes, we carry them. And sometimes, all we need is to know that someone else understands.

This publication isn't about having all the answers. It's about asking better questions, making room for difficult conversations, and finding meaning in the experiences we often struggle to put into words.

I'm Sanae, and this is A Place to Land.
